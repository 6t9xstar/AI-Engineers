# Cross Attention

## Definition
Attention mechanism where queries originate from one sequence (e.g. decoder) and keys/values from another (e.g. encoder).

## Architectural Intuition
In production AI engineering, **Cross Attention** represents a fundamental building block. Understanding its performance characteristics, failure modes, and mathematical properties ensures resilient system design.
